Here is a detailed prompt you can paste into Figma AI or Figma Make:

Design a complete, high-end e-commerce website for **Skin Protocol RX**, a family-owned professional skincare company created in New York.

## Brand Positioning

Skin Protocol RX is an organic-inspired, luxurious, high-performance skincare line built around a simple philosophy:

**No animal testing. No unnecessary additives. Just what your skin needs to look and feel healthy.**

The brand should feel:

* Luxurious but approachable
* Clinical but not sterile
* Natural but not rustic
* Professional enough for spas and estheticians
* Simple and trustworthy for everyday skincare customers
* Modern, clean, calm, refined, and results-focused

Avoid overly feminine pink beauty branding, busy botanical illustrations, harsh medical visuals, or generic “clean beauty” styling.

## Brand Colors

Use the following primary palette:

* **Signature Green:** #4D954C
* **Black:** #000000
* **Soft Ivory:** #F7F6F1
* **Warm White:** #FFFFFF
* **Light Sage:** #E8F0E6
* **Soft Gray:** #E8E8E8
* **Charcoal:** #252525

Use green selectively for buttons, links, icons, filters, badges, and important calls to action. Use black for strong typography and luxury contrast. Use ivory and white to keep the website spacious and sophisticated.

## Typography

Use an elegant editorial serif font for major headings paired with a clean modern sans-serif font for body copy, product information, filters, buttons, and account features.

Typography should feel premium and readable, with:

* Large editorial headlines
* Generous spacing
* Clean product names
* Small uppercase labels
* Strong but refined calls to action
* Clear pricing and product details

## Visual Direction

Use professional skincare product photography on simple ivory, white, black, or muted green backgrounds. Product images should have consistent lighting, scale, and positioning.

Use square product photography whenever possible, designed around a 1000 × 1000-pixel image ratio.

Include subtle visual elements such as:

* Soft shadows
* Frosted glass overlays
* Thin black divider lines
* Close-up ingredient textures
* Water, serum, cream, and oil textures
* Minimal New York-inspired editorial details
* Natural skin photography showing healthy, realistic skin
* Clean spa treatment environments

Do not use excessive leaves, flowers, wooden props, or imagery that makes the brand look homemade or inexpensive.

## Website Goals

The website must:

1. Sell Skin Protocol RX products directly to customers.
2. Help customers find products based on their skin type and concerns.
3. Explain why the formulas are simple, purposeful, and effective.
4. Build trust around the family-owned New York story.
5. Allow customers to create accounts and review order history.
6. Recruit spas, estheticians, and skincare professionals as partners.
7. Provide a separate spa partner login and application process.
8. Capture email subscribers.
9. Work beautifully on desktop, tablet, and mobile.
