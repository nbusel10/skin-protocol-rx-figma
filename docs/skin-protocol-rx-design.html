Working design. Color and type.

The colors and fonts on the working design. Headlines are Playfair Display. Everything else is DM Sans. Rose is reserved for emphasis, rules, and hover. Warm neutrals carry the page.

Color

Taken from the working pages: home, protocol, header, and footer. The lighter page uses the same inks and the same rose. Its ground is white, its labels are stone, and its buttons are hairline instead of filled.

Type

Two families. Playfair Display for display type, captions on photographs, and step numbers. DM Sans for body, navigation, buttons, and labels. Both are set from Google Fonts.
