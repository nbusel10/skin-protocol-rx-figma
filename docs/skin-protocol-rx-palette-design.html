Palette. Six colors.

The Palette tab uses this set and nothing else. Headlines are Playfair Display. Everything else is DM Sans. Rose Gold is reserved for emphasis, rules, and hover. Linen White is the page. Glossy White is the card, the header, and the type on Espresso Charcoal.

Color

Six colors cover the home, shop, product, protocol, quiz results, and about pages. Quieter labels are Espresso Charcoal at a lower opacity. That is the same color, drawn lighter.

Type

Two families, the same as the working design. Playfair Display for display type, photograph lines, and step numbers. DM Sans for body, navigation, buttons, and labels. Both are set from Google Fonts. On this tab, body, captions, and headlines share Espresso Charcoal.
